Freight CBM — Privacy Policy

Updated: November 5, 2026

Freight CBM is an offline cargo volume calculator. It does not require an account.

Information we collect

The app developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or store personal information, dimensions, or calculation history. Calculations take place on the device and are not saved when the app closes.

Clipboard

When you choose “Copy result,” the app writes the displayed calculation to your device clipboard. It does not read clipboard content.

Third-party services

The released app does not include advertisements, analytics trackers, or a server operated by the developer. App distribution and downloads are handled by Apple under its own policies.

Changes / Contact

Any material changes will be reflected on this page. For questions, see Support.
